# Queue 16 Phase 2: band-skip crosser outcomes — won vs lost (2026-08-31)

Script: `scripts/queue16_phase2_outcomes.py` (analysis only, read-only).
Follow-up to `docs/analysis/2026-08-31-queue16-bandskip.md` item 10: phase 1
measured how often band-skipped H-MAPENTRY1 evaluations CROSS INTO [0.50, 0.80]
within +120s (~39%). Crossing ≠ edge, so phase 2 joins each +120s crosser to
settlement truth to split **crossed-and-won** from **crossed-and-lost**.
Computed 2026-08-31 ~21:35 UTC against the live corpus; the capture daemon
appends today's rows, so counts drift by a handful at day's end (phase-1
population 4,282 at phase-1 time, ~4,294 at this run) — the resolved split was
stable at 667W/388L across repeated runs (crossers 1,066→1,067 as rows landed).

## Method

- **Population**: byte-identical to phase 1 (verified: zero key-set diff vs
  the phase-1 loader in one process) — band-skip evaluations with numeric
  p_map outside [0.50, 0.80], a mapped `market_slug`, `us_side` in {long,
  short}, valid t0 book, first evaluation per (series, map, round, slug,
  side). Crossers = rows whose our-side quote at +120s (nearest same-slug
  quote in [t0+105s, t0+150s]) is inside the band. Phase-1's loader drops
  map_number/team, so this script re-implements it locally carrying the join
  fields — population, dedup key and +120s sample are byte-identical.
- **Resolution truth** (in priority order, all read-only):
  1. Captured map resolution via `scripts/grid_winners.py` over
     `data/grid/series_state-*.jsonl` — the frozen first-observation
     map-winner oracle (MR12-validated, ambiguous finals excluded). This is
     how `src/gridbot/instruments/mapentry_verdict.py` resolves H-MAPENTRY1
     rows: these are MAP-winner books, so the map winner, not the round
     winner, is the settlement. Reused as a subprocess — pure consumer of the
     frozen oracle, no src/ touched.
  2. `data/shadow/instances-*.jsonl`: checked and rejected as an independent
     source — instance rows carry no resolution field (verdict.py derives
     resolved/won from the same grid capture), so they add zero coverage.
  3. `data/grid/` capture: raw source of 1, not a separate oracle.
- **Won**: the position settles in our favor iff OUR TEAM won the map, for
  BOTH legs. The venue names one team as the Yes side of a map book;
  `us_side` is long when our team is that named team and short when the
  OPPONENT is named (our team's win is then the No leg) — either way we bet
  our team wins, matching phase-1's our-side quote (P(our team wins) = mid /
  1−mid). Trace-verified against the oracle (e.g. a short-side row whose team
  won the map resolves won=True). `team` matching neither winner nor loser is
  unresolved, never guessed.
- **Settled edge per crossed side** = win_rate − crossed_price, where
  crossed_price = mean our-side quote at the +120s crossing (the price the
  band would have admitted). CI: series-cluster bootstrap, 1000 resamples,
  95% percentile, seed 16.

## Results

Crossers: 1,067 (104 series); resolved 1,055 (98.9% coverage); unresolved 12.

| stratum (pts outside band) | crossers | won | lost | win% (resolved) | crossed px | edge | edge CI |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ≤5 | 165 | 95 | 70 | 57.6% | 0.597 | −0.022 | [−0.106, +0.057] |
| 5–10 | 114 | 74 | 38 | 66.1% | 0.605 | +0.056 | [−0.022, +0.148] |
| >10 | 788 | 498 | 280 | 64.0% | 0.599 | +0.041 | [−0.014, +0.100] |
| **ALL** | **1,067** | **667** | **388** | **63.2%** | **0.599** | **+0.033** | **[−0.014, +0.083]** |

| side of band | crossers | won | lost | win% (resolved) | crossed px | edge | edge CI |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| below-band (p_map < 0.50) | 474 | 211 | 258 | 45.0% | 0.578 | −0.128 | [−0.219, −0.039] |
| above-band (p_map > 0.80) | 593 | 456 | 130 | 77.8% | 0.617 | +0.162 | [+0.089, +0.227] |

Median crossed price: won 60.5pt (n=667) vs lost 52.0pt (n=388).

## Read (describe-only, no rule-change claim)

1. Coverage is excellent: 1,055/1,067 crossers (98.9%) resolve in capture, so
   the won/lost split is not a coverage artifact; the 12 unresolved are maps
   that never produced a definitive MR12-valid winner.
2. **Headline: resolved crossers win 63.2% at a mean crossed price of 0.599 →
   settled edge +0.033, CI [−0.014, +0.083].** Crossing into the band is
   roughly break-even on expectation — the gate's rejections are not, by this
   measure, obviously leaving large money on the table.
3. **But the two sides of the band are opposite signs AND each is significant.**
   Above-band crossers (our team a favorite at p_map>0.80 that the market
   repriced DOWN into the band within 2 min) won 77.8% at 0.617 → **+0.162
   edge, CI [+0.089, +0.227]**. Below-band crossers (our team an underdog that
   the market repriced UP into the band) won only 45.0% at 0.578 → **−0.128
   edge, CI [−0.219, −0.039]**. Pooling the two sides hides two significant,
   opposed effects.
4. Direction of the read: a favorite's quick fall into the band is mostly
   over-pessimism — the team still wins 78% of those maps at a ~0.62 entry;
   an underdog's quick rise is mostly over-optimism — it still loses 55% of
   the time at a ~0.58 entry. Winners cost more (60.5pt median) and lose-cost
   less (52.0pt) — the edge is buying the fallen favorite, not the woken
   longshot.
5. Distance gradient is flat and non-monotone (≤5 −0.022, 5–10 +0.056, >10
   +0.041): how deep the skip was does not drive value; which side of the band
   it was on does.
6. Caveat A — price reading: crossed price is the +120s our-side MID, not an
   executable entry. The entry would trigger when the quote first crosses
   ≤0.80 (likely at a higher price than the +120s sample) and would pay the
   ASK, not the mid. Both bias executable edge DOWN from the reported +0.162 /
   −0.128, especially in the venue's thin tops.
7. Caveat B — single-sample crossing: +120s is one nearest-quote sample
   (phase-1 method), not "crossed and stayed"; short-lived crossings would
   resolve differently, and the +0.162 would not survive an ask-price +
   persistence filter intact.
8. Caveat C — do NOT read this as contradicting H-PRICEDIN1: that verdict
   covers qualifying fills inside the band (executed trades); this is the
   band-SKIPPED population that crossed in within 2 min, describe-only, with
   mid-priced crosses. Different population, different price, and this report
   makes no rule claim.
9. No claim against the frozen band, no registry edit, nothing in src/: this
   is describe-only evidence. It does not show a widening would have profited
   (the executable-price caveat is material), and it is one of several
   measurements a widening amendment would need.
10. Suggested next measurement (not run): price an above-band-only widening
    on executable ASK at first cross-below-0.80 with a persistence filter
    (crossed and stayed), over the BOOKPIN1 holdout, before any registry
    amendment is drafted.
