## Band gate behaved exactly as designed (harm reduction, not edge)

Every one of the 18 `skipped:band_gate` intents had **p_map > 0.80**
(range 0.8053–0.9912). Zero in-band (0.50–0.80) qualifying rows were gated
out. Rationale confirmed live: on both maps the model sat **above 0.80 most
of the time** (that is where the −8..−9.5pt measured harm lives), so the
in-band window (0.50–0.80) was brief and produced few placements. Instances
where p_map stayed in the risky band did NOT get armed — the exclusion is
doing its job, and the pre-registered "thin in-band ledger" prediction held.

read; there is also nothing with statistical weight yet (n=3 crossings, 2
maps). Fill probability at fair−4pts on PMUS map books is *effectively zero
in this sample* — keep measuring, do not change entry/TTL on this evidence.

## TTL cancels in the wild

The 150s TTL fired cleanly on all 3 (151.4–151.6s, right at the target). No
failed cancels, no manual-action escapes. First real-world confirmation the
stale-maker cancel pass works. Operational observation: for a **maker** quote
on a **thin map book**, 150s is aggressive — the book must travel the
≥4pt gap TO our resting price within 150s; tonight it never did. This is the STATE.md #11
TTL-calibration open item; record as a revisit candidate, not an unregistered
change (any TTL change is a user/registered decision).

## Reconcile drift — all noise, zero true anomalies

156 reconcile rows over the session. Breakdown:
- `gone_from_venue` reprints: C3RBPBKHTFSK ×143, C3SGA4ENPFSQ ×65,
  C3SJWSQJJFSM ×55 — **every one disproven** by the later clean cancel
  (status "cancelled", not "gone_before_cancel").
- `unknown_on_venue`: **0** (the real single-account anomaly — none). Good.
- `partial_fills`: **0**.
- `ledger_open` climbed to 3 and never decremented — the documented
  held-slot artifact (no fill→settle writer exists; cancelled slots are
  never released). Actual concurrent resting exposure never exceeded 2
  (order3 placed after order2's cancel).

CONFIRMED across all three orders: the reconcile false-alarm in STATE.md #1b
is **systemic, not a one-off fetch error on order1**. `fetch_venue_orders`
returning [] on any error reads as "venue empty" → phantom `gone_from_venue`
on every cycle, firing the night-alert each time. Direction was safe
(false alarm only; TTL cancel is the real guard), but the volume (156 rows,
night-alert spam) is worth the #1b fix: return None on error + skip the diff,
and/or require 2 consecutive gone sightings. This is the single most
actionable follow-up from the armed session.

## Ops: executor daemon left down after armed handoff (Hermes restored)

`gridbot-executor.service` was in `failed` (status 143 = SIGTERM, a *clean*
stop, not a crash) since **01:52 PDT** — stopped for the armed session per
runbook but never restarted after Ctrl-C, leaving the dry-run intent ledger
(guard rail) offline ~2h44m. **Hermes restarted it 04:37 PDT and verified

after Ctrl-C" step is easy to miss at 02:00 after an all-night session.
Consider `systemd-inhibit`/a timer or a scripted disarm that auto-restarts
the daemon, so the guard rail is never silently absent.
